TRAINING FAQ

Before you apply.

Helpful answers for prospective learners. Exact schedules and fees will be published after confirmation by HAZMA ICT.

Can beginners register?

Yes. The training catalogue includes beginner-friendly options, and learners can indicate their current experience level during registration.

How do I choose a course?

Review the learning outcomes on each course card and submit the training registration form. HAZMA ICT can then confirm the suitable programme and schedule.

Are course fees shown online?

Not yet. We will publish official fees and durations only after HAZMA ICT confirms them.

How will I know my registration was received?

The current website demonstrates the registration flow. Confirmation messaging and staff notifications will become live when the backend is connected.
